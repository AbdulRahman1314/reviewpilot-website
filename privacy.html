Privacy Policy

ReviewPilot ([your company or your name], “we”) is a tool that helps local businesses ask customers for honest feedback. This page explains what information is handled and why.

Who is who

The business that uses ReviewPilot decides which customers to contact and for what reason. ReviewPilot stores and processes this information on the business’s behalf.

What we collect

  • Business owners: name, email, hashed password, business name, and an optional Google review link.
  • Customers (entered by the business): mobile number, optional name, bill number and product, and a record that the customer agreed to receive messages.
  • Customer feedback: the star rating, the written comment, the topic chosen, and whether the customer is happy to be contacted privately.
  • Clicks: we count when the Google button is pressed. We do not know whether a Google review was posted.

Why we use it

  • To create a personal feedback link and show the business what its customers said.
  • To stop messages to customers who have opted out, and to avoid repeat requests.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not post anything to Google for a customer. Google reviews are written and submitted by the customer on Google.
  • We do not show a customer’s full phone number in reports.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with service providers: a database hosted by Neon (United States) and a server hosted by Render (Singapore). Feedback pages are served through GitHub Pages. These providers handle data under their own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep data until the business deletes it or asks us to, or until the account is closed. [Add your retention period.]

Your choices

A customer can reply STOP to any message to opt out, or ask the business to remove their data. A business can mark a customer as opted out inside the app. See Data Deletion below.

Terms of Service

  • Permission first. You may only message customers who have agreed to receive messages from your business. You must confirm this in the app before each request.
  • Honest feedback only. Do not offer rewards, discounts or gifts for Google reviews. Do not write reviews for customers. Do not hide the Google option from customers who give low ratings.
  • No misuse. Do not send spam, abusive or illegal content, or use the service to harass anyone.
  • Your account. Keep your password safe. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • No promises. ReviewPilot does not guarantee more reviews, higher ratings or better Google ranking.
  • Availability. The service is provided as is and may be unavailable at times. During testing it can be slow after a period of inactivity.
  • Suspension. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
