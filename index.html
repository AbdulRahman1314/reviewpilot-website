WhatsApp, done by the rules

Consent first

You cannot send a request until you confirm the customer agreed to receive messages. Opted-out customers are blocked.

Two ways to send

Share the link yourself from your own WhatsApp today. Automatic sending uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform once you connect it.

Automatic WhatsApp needs approved message templates, and Meta charges for messages. You will see the limits and charges before you switch it on.

Fair by design

No review gating

Customers who give low ratings see the same Google invitation as everyone else.

No incentives, no fake reviews

We never help write reviews for customers or reward them for posting one.

Clear about Google

A review is only counted as published when it is verified. A click is never treated as a review.

Privacy and security

Each business sees only its own data. Phone numbers are masked in reports, and secrets stay on the server.

Pricing plans

Frequently asked questions

Will this increase my Google rating?

We cannot promise that. ReviewPilot makes it easier to ask for honest feedback, and customers choose what to write.

Does a feedback rating count as a Google review?

No. Feedback given on ReviewPilot is private to your business. Google reviews are submitted separately on Google.

Can I message every number I have?

No. You must have the customer’s permission, and the app records it. Customers can opt out at any time.

Do I need WhatsApp Business API?

Not to start. You can share links from your own WhatsApp or use a QR code. API is only needed for automatic sending.

Does it work on a basic Android phone?

Yes. The app is designed phone-first, and the customer page is light and fast.
